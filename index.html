Questions

What owners usually ask first.

Why is the concept free?

Because showing is faster than explaining. I only build a concept when I already see what your site is missing, and most owners decide within a minute once they see their own business on a phone.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing. I take photos, services and contacts from your current site, Facebook page or catalogue. If you have better photos, send them later.

What exactly do I get for 300 €?

The full site, up to 5 pages, on your domain: mobile-first, with your texts and photos, all files and source code, and a month of free edits after launch.

Are there monthly fees?

No. 300 € once. The domain is renewed with your registrar as usual, and hosting for a site like this can be free.

How long does it take?

The concept: a day or two. The full site after your yes: usually within a week.

Do we have to talk on the phone?

No. Everything can be done in writing, in your language: email, WhatsApp, Viber or Telegram. If you prefer a call, that is fine too.

What if I say no?

Then you pay nothing. The concept is marked as unofficial, and I take it down if you ask.

You are in Ukraine. Is that a risk?

Your site and files live on hosting outside Ukraine, so nothing depends on my power grid. And I answer within a day.
